Our Story

About Ivy Africa

Founded with a vision to bridge the digital divide, Ivy Africa has grown into a trusted partner for organisations across the continent.

Timeline

Our Journey

2019

Ivy Africa Founded

Established in Johannesburg with a focus on IT training and certification.

2020

First Major Partnership

Secured Dell Technologies partnership and expanded into hardware solutions.

2021

Training Division Launch

Launched accredited training programmes with CompTIA, PECB, and ISACA.

2022

SMME & Consultancy

Added SMME development and IT consultancy divisions.

2023

Ed-Tech Expansion

Entered the education technology market with K12 and higher ed solutions.

2024

Pan-African Growth

Expanded operations into neighbouring countries and broadened partner network.

2025

Full-Spectrum Partner

Recognised as a leading technology and training partner across six divisions.
