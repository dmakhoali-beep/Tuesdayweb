Our Offerings

Seven Pillars for Basic Education

We partner with schools and districts to analyse their digital vision and deliver holistic technology solutions that improve learning outcomes from Grade R to Matric.

📚

Learning Supplies

Digital and print resources for the CAPS curriculum. E-readers, interactive textbooks, supplementary readers, and assessment banks for Foundation to FET phases.

💻

Learning Technology

Interactive smartboards, classroom projectors, learner tablets, computer labs, coding & robotics kits, and STEM equipment for hands-on discovery.

🤖

LLMs & Artificial Intelligence

AI tutoring assistants, automated marking for formative assessments, personalised learning pathways, and early-warning systems to identify at-risk learners.

🎓

Talent Development

Teacher training in digital pedagogy, SACE-accredited CPTD courses, ICT integration workshops, and leadership development for school management teams.

🏗️

Enterprise Infrastructure

School network connectivity, Wi-Fi coverage, server rooms, cloud-based administration, and cybersecurity to protect learner data and school records.

📊

School Management Systems

Integrated platforms for admissions, attendance, timetabling, parent communication, fee management, and district reporting to the Department of Education.

🌐

Digital Transformation

Strategic planning to turn every school into a smart school. We bring together the best global EdTech to match your budget, context, and ambition.
